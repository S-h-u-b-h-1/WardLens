## Prepared project description

**Title:** WardLens — multilingual civic evidence intelligence.

**Problem:** People describe local infrastructure issues in different languages, but short scattered observations are difficult to compare. Geographical proximity can easily be misrepresented as an established common cause.

**Users:** Residents, community volunteers and local civic investigators working with unverified infrastructure observations.

**Solution:** A user consents to a short voice recording through Gnani Prisma v2.5 speech-to-text, reviews and corrects the transcript, places a map pin and saves an observation privately in their browser. A conservative extractive-first engine identifies supported symptoms, requests clarification where needed and displays *possible* links on an interactive OpenStreetMap evidence graph with exact source quotes. The map includes search, a GPS-accuracy-aware 300 m proximity estimate, and an honest Delhi fallback when geolocation is unavailable.

**Actual Gnani model used:** Prisma v2.5 speech-to-text via the official Gnani API. Do not claim Timbre, Evon, municipal integrations, server-side cloud report sharing, proven causal inference, or successful live speech tests that were not done.

**Contribution:** Credit the actual developer(s) and describe individual work truthfully.
